🤖 AI CLOSER SETUP CHECKLIST — STOP LOSING LEADS

Every slow reply is money walking to your competitor.
Run these 4 checks in order. Each takes 15-30 minutes.

CHECK 1 — REPLY IN 60 SECONDS (the AI closer)
Speed-to-lead is the whole game. Set this up today:
- Connect every lead source (IG DM, WhatsApp, website form,
  Meta lead ads) to ONE inbox you actually check.
- Turn on an instant auto-reply: "Got it! Checking the details,
  will reply in 2 minutes." — then actually reply in 2 minutes.
- Build your AI closer: one Claude Project (or WhatsApp Business
  quick replies) with your prices, offers, and answers to your 10
  most-asked questions pasted in. It drafts, you tap send.
- Night mode: after-hours leads get an instant reply + a next-day
  priority slot. Never let a lead sleep unanswered.

CHECK 2 — FOLLOW UP ON AUTOPILOT (Day 1, 3, 7)
80% of sales need 5+ follow-ups. Nobody follows up. That is your edge:
- Day 1 (1 hour after first reply): "Saw my message? Any question?"
- Day 3: send one useful thing — a site photo, a client review,
  a clear price breakdown.
- Day 7: the nudge — "Should I hold your slot for this week?"
- Save these 3 as templates. Every cold lead gets all 3.
  No exceptions, no "I'll do it tomorrow."

CHECK 3 — BOOK THE VISIT (calendar does the selling)
Stop the "sir time batao" back-and-forth:
- Make one booking link (Google Calendar booking page or Calendly)
  with 2-3 slot options. Send it in your very first reply.
- Auto-confirm: when they book, they get the address + a reminder
  2 hours before. No-shows drop hard.
- Missed call = instant text with the booking link. Always.

CHECK 4 — ZERO-SLIP CRM (every lead tracked)
A lead you cannot see is a lead you lost:
- One Google Sheet: Name | Source | Status | Last contact |
  Next follow-up.
- 4 statuses only: New / Talking / Visit booked / Closed (won-lost).
- Every morning, 2 minutes: sort by "Next follow-up" and work the list.
- Rule: no lead sits 48 hours without a status update. Ever.

START TODAY (45 minutes total):
1. Write your 10 most-asked questions + answers (15 min).
2. Set instant auto-reply on your main lead channel (10 min).
3. Save the 3 follow-up templates (10 min).
4. Make your booking link, put it in your first reply (10 min).

That is the whole AI closer system from the reel.
