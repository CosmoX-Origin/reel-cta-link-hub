# Content Machine Setup Checklist — ENGINE

One AI workspace that plans, writes, builds and publishes content while you
focus on the business. Build it once, then run it every day.

## Step 1 — One workspace, not ten apps

- Create one Claude Project and name it `CONTENT ENGINE`.
- Paste this as the Project Instructions:

> You are my content engine. Help me plan, write, and publish content in
> my voice. Be direct, no fluff. Always ask for missing info before
> guessing. Never invent prices, results, or quotes.

- Put your business info in the project once: what you sell, who buys it,
  your offers, your tone, and 3 examples of content you liked.

## Step 2 — Pick 3 content pillars

- Pillar 1: Teach — how-to guides and mistakes to avoid.
- Pillar 2: Proof — results, client stories, and behind-the-scenes work.
- Pillar 3: Offer — your service or product, explained simply.
- Every post must fit one pillar. If it fits none, do not post it.

## Step 3 — Build an idea bank

- Keep one list: 30 ideas, 10 per pillar.
- Add to it whenever a customer asks a question. A question is a post.
- Every Sunday, pick next week's 5 ideas from the bank. No daily
  "what should I post?" panic.

## Step 4 — The daily production line (30 minutes)

1. Plan (5 min): pick today's idea and one message only.
2. Write (15 min): hook first, one point, one call to action. No essays.
3. Design (5 min): one clean visual, one headline, brand colours only.
4. Publish (5 min): post it, save the idea and the result in your bank.

## Step 5 — Reuse everything

- One idea = one reel + one carousel + one short post. Same message,
  three formats, three days.
- Every month, repost your best performer with a fresh visual. Good
  content deserves a second run.

## Daily checklist

- [ ] Today's one idea picked
- [ ] Hook written first, not last
- [ ] Visual made, headline checked
- [ ] Post published and saved in the bank
- [ ] One customer question added to the idea bank

That is the whole engine. One workspace, three pillars, one idea bank,
30 minutes a day.
