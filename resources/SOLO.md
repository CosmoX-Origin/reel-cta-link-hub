🤖 RUN A 1-PERSON COMPANY WITH AI — FULL GUIDE

No team. No payroll. Just you + AI employees.
Do this in order. Each step takes 10-20 minutes.

STEP 1 — HIRE YOUR FIRST AI EMPLOYEE (start with SALES)
Go to claude.ai → Projects → New Project → name it "SALES REP".
Paste this as the Project Instructions:

"You are my AI sales rep. Be direct, no fluff. Your job: qualify
leads, write follow-ups, and draft proposals in my voice. Always ask
for missing info before guessing. Never invent prices or promises —
use only what I tell you."

Give it ONE real job today: "Here's my last 5 customer chats. Write
a follow-up message for each lead that went cold."
One job, done well, beats ten half-built automations.

STEP 2 — PLUG IN YOUR TOOLS
An AI employee without tools is just a chatbot. Connect:
- Gmail → it reads and drafts your email
- Google Calendar → it knows your schedule
- Notion / Google Drive → it reads your docs, SOPs, price lists
- Slack / WhatsApp → it drafts your replies
- Your CRM (or a simple Google Sheet) → it logs every lead

In Claude: Settings → Connectors → link Google Drive and Gmail.
Rule: the AI never sends anything by itself. It drafts, you tap send.

STEP 3 — TEACH IT YOUR BUSINESS (show once, it learns forever)
In the project chat, paste these five things ONCE:
- What you sell (products / services + prices)
- Who buys from you (your exact customer)
- Your tone of voice (paste 3 of your best messages)
- Your offers and guarantees
- Your SOPs — how you actually handle a lead, step by step

Example opener: "Here's everything about my business. Read it,
then summarize back what you understood. Ask me what's missing."
That summary becomes your AI's permanent brain.

STEP 4 — AUTOMATE EVERYTHING (schedule it, clone the winners)
Pick the ONE task you repeat daily and automate it first:
- Morning: "Check my inbox, list the 5 emails needing replies,
  draft each reply." Run it every morning at 9 AM.
- Leads: every new enquiry gets a qualification reply in under
  5 minutes (drafted by AI, sent by you).
- Winners: when a workflow saves you 30+ min/week, CLONE it —
  duplicate the Project for the next job (support, content, ops).

Rule of thumb: if you do it twice, document it. If you do it
five times, automate it.

STEP 5 — BUILD YOUR AI DREAM TEAM
Once one employee works, hire the rest. Each gets its own
Claude Project with its own instructions:
- 🔍 RESEARCH — "Summarize any topic in 5 bullets + 3 sources."
- 📣 MARKETING — "Write in my voice. Hook first, CTA last."
- 💰 SALES — your first hire (Step 1).
- ⚙️ OPS — "Turn my messy notes into checklists and SOPs."
- 📊 FINANCE — "Track income/expenses from my pasted data,
  flag leaks monthly."
- 🧠 CHIEF OF STAFF — "You coordinate the team. Every morning,
  tell me the 3 highest-leverage things to do today."

You handle decisions only. AI does the rest.

Start today: hire ONE (sales), connect TWO tools (Gmail + Drive),
teach it your business ONCE. That's the whole system.
