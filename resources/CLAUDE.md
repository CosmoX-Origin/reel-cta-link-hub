🤖 CLAUDE STARTER GUIDE — YOUR 60-MINUTE AI EMPLOYEE

STEP 1 — THINK TEAMMATE, NOT TOOL (0-10 min)
Talk to Claude like a new hire. Give it the goal, let it figure out the how.
Bad: "write a marketing email"
Good: "You're my marketing lead. We sell [your product] to [your customers].
Write a launch email — casual tone, under 150 words, one clear CTA."

STEP 2 — PROMPT WITH A FORMULA (10-20 min)
Every good prompt has 5 parts:
ROLE + GOAL + CONTEXT + RULES + OUTPUT

Copy-paste this template:
"Act as [ROLE].
My goal is [GOAL].
Here's the context: [paste your business info].
Rules: [tone, length, what to avoid].
Give me [OUTPUT format: bullets / table / script]."

STEP 3 — GIVE IT YOUR BRAIN (20-30 min)
In any Claude chat, paste these five things once:
- What you sell (products / services)
- Who buys from you (customers)
- Your offers and prices
- Your tone of voice
- Your SOPs (how you actually work)
Context is what turns a chatbot into YOUR assistant.

STEP 4 — BUILD YOUR BUSINESS OS (30-40 min)
Go to claude.ai -> Projects -> New Project -> name it "Business OS".
Paste your business info as the Project Instructions:
"You are my business partner. Help me with sales, marketing and
operations. Be direct, no fluff. Always ask for missing info before
guessing."
Run one small task first, check the output, then go bigger.

STEP 5 — TOOLS ON. EMPLOYEE HIRED (40-60 min)
Connect: Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Drive, Notion, n8n.
Then give it ONE real job today, for example:
"Summarize my inbox from today and list the 5 emails that need a reply,
with a draft reply for each."

That's it. 60 minutes. One AI employee that never sleeps.
