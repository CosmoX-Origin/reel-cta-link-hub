🤖 YOUR OWN AI COACH — FULL SETUP GUIDE

STEP 1 — CREATE YOUR COACH
Go to claude.ai → Projects → New Project → name it "AI COACH".
Paste this as the Project Instructions:

"You are my personal AI coach. Be direct and practical, no fluff.
Design workouts around my goals, fitness level, injuries and schedule.
Ask for any missing info before planning. Track my progress across
sessions and adjust the plan as I improve. Motivate like a coach,
not a textbook."

STEP 2 — TEACH IT YOUR BODY
In the project chat, tell it everything once:
- Goal (fat loss / muscle / stamina)
- Age, weight, height
- Fitness level (beginner / intermediate / advanced)
- Injuries or pain points
- Weekly schedule (which days, how many minutes)
- Equipment you have (none / dumbbells / full gym)
Example: "I'm 28, 82kg, beginner. Goal: lose belly fat. 30 min/day,
Mon–Fri. No equipment. Lower back pain sometimes."

STEP 3 — CONNECT YOUR GEAR
Claude can't read your watch directly, so feed it the data:
- Strava / Garmin / Apple Health / Whoop: export the week's summary
  (or screenshot the stats) and drop it in the project chat every Sunday.
- Say: "Here's my training, sleep and HRV data for the week. Adjust
  next week's plan and flag anything wrong."

STEP 4 — GIVE IT A MEMORY
Keep one note (Obsidian / Notion / even a text file) called
"Coach Memory" with: current plan, PRs, body weight (weekly),
sleep average, injuries.
Paste it into the project at the start of each week:
"Read my Coach Memory first, then plan this week."

STEP 5 — TRAIN SMARTER DAILY
Every morning, message your coach:
"Slept 6h, legs sore. Today's plan?"
Every Sunday: "Weekly review — what improved, what do I fix next week?"
That loop is the whole system: plan → train → log → review → adjust.
